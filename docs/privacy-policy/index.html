AIDA — Analytical Intelligent Diagnostic Agent

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 28, 2026 · Last updated: August 28, 2026

Developer: AIDA Labs, an independent software development project · Contact: AIDAdeveloper@outlook.com

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how AIDA Labs handles information in connection with AIDA — the Analytical Intelligent Diagnostic Agent — including the Android application distributed under package identifier com.aida.agent and related AIDA service components operated for the application.

AIDA is currently in Early Alpha development. Features, data flows, and service providers may change as development continues. This policy will be updated when those changes materially affect privacy practices.

2. Our privacy approach

AIDA is designed to perform as much device interaction and diagnostic work locally as practical. AIDA Labs does not sell personal information, does not operate advertising within AIDA, and does not use AIDA data to build advertising profiles.

Some features require remote processing, including AI reasoning, speech transcription, and speech synthesis. When a remote feature is used, the minimum information reasonably needed to perform that feature may be transmitted away from the device.

3. Information stored locally on the device

AIDA may store the following information locally on the user's device:

  • a randomly generated AIDA instance identifier used to distinguish that local installation;
  • runtime preferences such as speech and autonomy settings;
  • local activity and diagnostic history generated by AIDA;
  • service-gateway configuration information; and
  • temporary audio files created while voice features are being used.

Local storage alone is not treated by AIDA Labs as off-device collection. Local information remains on the device unless a feature specifically sends relevant information for remote processing, the user explicitly submits it, or disclosure is otherwise described in this policy.

4. Information that may be transmitted for remote processing

Depending on the feature used, AIDA may transmit the following categories of information:

  • User communications: text typed or spoken to AIDA, generated transcripts, and a limited amount of recent conversation context used to produce relevant responses.
  • Voice audio: microphone recordings created when the user intentionally invokes voice input.
  • Device and application context: information such as operating-system platform and version, device model, supported AIDA capabilities, and a pseudonymous AIDA instance identifier.
  • Requested technical context: diagnostic or system information when it is necessary for a user-requested remote reasoning function or when the user includes that information in a request.
  • Speech content: text that AIDA sends for voice synthesis so a spoken response can be produced.

5. How information is used

AIDA Labs processes information only for purposes related to operating, securing, testing, and improving AIDA, including:

  • interpreting user requests and producing AIDA responses;
  • routing requests to local or remote AIDA capabilities;
  • performing requested diagnostics and technical analysis;
  • transcribing user-initiated voice input;
  • generating AIDA speech output;
  • maintaining local runtime state and continuity;
  • detecting failures, abuse, or security problems; and
  • evaluating Early Alpha functionality and reliability.

6. Service providers

AIDA Labs may use specialized service providers to perform functions on AIDA's behalf. Depending on the enabled configuration, these may include:

  • Microsoft Azure OpenAI for AI reasoning and language-model processing;
  • OpenAI for speech transcription services; and
  • ElevenLabs for synthesized speech output.

Information sent to these providers is limited to what is needed for the requested function. Those providers may process data under their own contractual terms, security practices, and retention controls. AIDA Labs does not authorize these providers to use AIDA user data for advertising on AIDA Labs' behalf.

Google Play and the user's device platform may separately process installation, account, purchase, security, or diagnostic information under Google's or the device provider's own terms and privacy policies. AIDA Labs does not necessarily receive all information processed independently by those platforms.

7. Data sharing and sale

AIDA Labs does not sell user data. AIDA Labs does not share user data with advertisers or data brokers. Information may be disclosed to service providers described above when required to perform an AIDA function, or when reasonably necessary to comply with law, protect users, investigate abuse, or protect the security and integrity of AIDA and its infrastructure.

8. Voice data and temporary files

Voice recording begins only when the user intentionally invokes AIDA's voice-input function and grants the required microphone permission. AIDA's mobile client is designed to delete its temporary local microphone recording after transcription succeeds or fails.

Audio generated for AIDA's synthesized response may also be written temporarily to application cache for playback and is designed to be deleted after playback completes. Deleting a local temporary file does not necessarily mean a remote service provider has no independent short-term retention under its applicable service configuration.

9. Retention

Device-local AIDA state may remain on the device until it is cleared by the user, removed through operating-system controls, reset by an AIDA feature, or deleted when the application is uninstalled, subject to the behavior of the operating system and backups.

The current AIDA Services Gateway is intended to process requests as a service boundary rather than function as a permanent cloud conversation archive. Third-party AI, transcription, and speech providers may retain request data for limited periods according to the provider configuration, contractual terms, abuse-monitoring requirements, or legal obligations applicable to the service in use.

10. Security

AIDA Labs uses technical and organizational measures intended to reduce unauthorized access to AIDA data. Service credentials are designed to remain outside distributed application bundles, and sensitive mobile gateway session information is stored using platform-provided secure storage where supported.

Early Alpha developer configurations may use local development service endpoints for testing. Such configurations are intended only for controlled testing environments. Broader production service deployment is intended to use authenticated, encrypted network transport. No system can guarantee absolute security, and Early Alpha testers should avoid using development endpoints across untrusted networks.

11. User choices and deletion

Users can decline microphone permission and continue using non-voice features that do not require it. Users can clear AIDA's local application data or uninstall the application to remove locally stored AIDA information, subject to device backup behavior.

AIDA does not currently require a general consumer account. If you believe AIDA Labs holds information about you outside your device and you want to request access, correction, or deletion, contact AIDAdeveloper@outlook.com. We may need enough information to verify and locate the relevant data before acting on a request.

12. Children

AIDA is a technical diagnostic application intended for an adult testing and technology audience. It is not directed to children, and AIDA Labs does not knowingly design the service to collect personal information from children. If AIDA Labs learns that child data has been collected contrary to this policy, we will take reasonable steps to address it.

13. Changes to this policy

AIDA Labs may update this Privacy Policy as AIDA develops, including when new Engines, diagnostics, cloud services, account features, telemetry, or platform integrations materially change how information is handled. The effective or last-updated date at the top of this page will be revised when the policy changes.
